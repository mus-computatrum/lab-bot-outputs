# Largest cortical single-cell ATAC-seq datasets in primates

**Scope:** single-cell/single-nucleus ATAC-seq (chromatin accessibility) from primate cortex.
Literature screened via PubMed 2020-2026. Ranked by reported nuclei. All "biggest" datasets
to date are HUMAN; non-human-primate (NHP) cortical ATAC remains limited to comparative M1/PFC studies.

## Top datasets by scale (human)
1. **Xiong et al. 2025, Cell (PMID 40752494)** - multi-region AD epigenomic atlas (ROSMAP/Kellis).
   ~3.5M cells (single-cell epigenomic + transcriptomic), 6 brain regions (incl. PFC & other neocortex),
   111 individuals / 384 samples, >1M cCREs. Paired snATAC+snRNA; snATAC is a large fraction. Public (Synapse/AD Knowledge Portal).
2. **PD long-read multiome 2026, bioRxiv (PMID 41889906)** - 3.1M snATAC nuclei (+1.1M snRNA),
   anterior cingulate cortex + substantia nigra, 190 donors. Largest explicitly-snATAC count, but ~half is cortex (ACC); preprint, accession pending.
3. **Emani et al. 2024, Science - PsychENCODE2/brainSCOPE (PMID 38781369)** - >2.8M PFC nuclei across 388
   donors (multiomics; snATAC a subset), >550k regulatory elements. Public (PsychENCODE/Synapse).
4. **Li et al. 2023, Science - catlas (PMID 37824643)** - 1.1M snATAC nuclei, 42 brain regions (many neocortical),
   3 adults, 544,735 cCREs, 107 cell types. THE largest dedicated snATAC-ONLY human brain atlas. Public (catlas.org/humanbrain).

## Non-human-primate cortical ATAC (the only options)
- **Zemke et al. 2023, Nature (PMID 38092918)** - comparative M1 multiome (human, MACAQUE, MARMOSET, mouse);
  >200k cells total across species/modalities. The single best resource for NHP cortical chromatin accessibility.
- **Bakken et al. 2021, Nature BICCN M1 (PMID 34616062/34616075)** - human + marmoset M1 multimodal census.
- **Developing macaque PFC multiome 2025, Dev Cell (PMID 39481377)** - NHP developmental cortex ATAC.

## Developmental / smaller human
- Trevino 2021 Cell (PMID 34390642), Ziffra 2021 Nature (PMID 34616060): fetal cortex multiome/snATAC (tens of thousands).
- Corces 2020 Nat Genet (PMID 33106633): ~70k snATAC, adult brain incl. cortex.

## Bottom line
For sheer scale, the field is human-dominated: Xiong 2025 (~3.5M multiomic) and the 2026 PD preprint (3.1M snATAC)
are the largest, followed by Emani 2024 (2.8M) and Li 2023 (1.1M snATAC-only). If you need a clean, cortex-rich,
snATAC-only atlas, Li 2023 (catlas) is the most usable. For ANY non-human primate cortical ATAC, Zemke 2023 is
effectively the only large-scale option (macaque + marmoset M1), with the developing-macaque PFC study as a complement.
